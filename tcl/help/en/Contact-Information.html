Links

Chesswiz users may find the following websites interesting:

  • ftp://ftp.cis.uab.edu/pub/hyatt/ 
    The legendary chess engine Crafty. The subdirectory there named TB has many Nalimov-format tablebase files that you can use in several chess programs and in Chesswiz.

  • tablebase.sesse.net 
    This site does also provide tablebase files, including 6-men bases.

  • The Week in Chess 
    An excellent weekly bulletin of international chess games in available in PGN format.

  • Standard: Portable Game Notation Specification and Implementation Guide 
    The PGN Standard, created by Steven J. Edwards in 1994. This website explains the PGN and EPD formats in detail.

  • ECO-Code 
    An overview about the ECO code.
