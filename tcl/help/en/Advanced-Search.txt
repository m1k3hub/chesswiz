TITLE Advanced Search

<p>
Advanced Search filters the games of one database. Combine any of the
fields; an empty field is ignored. The result list below shows the
matching games, and the title bar shows the active filter.
</p>

<ul>
	<li><b>Player</b> and <b>Player side</b>: a player playing either
	colour, White only or Black only. A plain surname such as
	<i>Carlsen</i> also finds <i>Carlsen, Magnus</i>.</li>
	<li><b>Event</b>, <b>Site</b>, <b>Annotator</b>: names of the
	event, site and annotator.</li>
	<li><b>Position ID</b>: the start position of the game.
	<b>Use Start Position</b> takes it from the current game.</li>
	<li><b>Rating</b>, <b>Mode</b>, <b>Range</b>: a rating type and
	a range such as 2200 or 2000-2400, for White, Black, the average
	of both, or White and Black separately (second range).</li>
	<li><b>Material</b>: Q/R/B/N letters plus the pawn count for White
	and Black, such as qrbn8. The mode matches exactly, the same
	majors and minors, or a pawn count range such as 5-7.</li>
</ul>

<p>
In name fields, * matches any text and ? one character. Prefix a name
with = to match it literally.
</p>

<p>
<b>Search</b> (or Return) runs the search, <b>Reset</b> clears all
fields. <b>Open In Switcher</b> shows the matching games in the
database switcher, and <b>Save Working Set...</b> stores them under a
name. Presets save and reload a set of field values.
</p>

END
