# Code of Conduct

Chesswiz follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, criticise ideas rather than people, and assume good intent. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and sharing others' private information are not accepted in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions or other spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at thomas.g.mike+chesswizapp@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break this code.
